Privacy Policy for ProxoPACS

Effective Date: October 16, 2026 • Application: ProxoPACS (Android / Web) • Developer: Proxomind Labs

Proxomind Labs ("we," "our," or "us") provides cloud-based medical imaging, PACS, AI assistance, and radiology workflow software, including the ProxoPACS mobile and web application. This Privacy Policy explains how information is collected, used, processed, and safeguarded when you use our mobile application and associated diagnostic platform.

1. Protected Health Information (PHI) & HIPAA Compliance

As a provider of enterprise medical imaging software, ProxoPACS processes Protected Health Information (PHI) and DICOM medical files strictly on behalf of our healthcare provider customers (hospitals, diagnostic imaging centers, clinics, and reading radiologists).

We act as a Business Associate or Data Processor under the Health Insurance Portability and Accountability Act (HIPAA) and applicable global data protection regulations. We do not own patient health data, nor do we sell, monetize, or use patient data for commercial marketing.

2. Information Collected by ProxoPACS

The ProxoPACS mobile application collects and processes only the data required to deliver secure diagnostic PACS functions:

  • Microphone & Audio Data (android.permission.RECORD_AUDIO):
    ProxoPACS requests permission to access your device's microphone exclusively for real-time speech dictation into medical report text fields. Speech recognition is processed strictly on-demand to transcribe your voice into clinical report impressions. Audio files are not stored permanently, sold, or shared with third parties.
  • Medical Imaging & Clinical Diagnostic Data:
    DICOM medical image series, study metadata (modality, study date, accession number, patient ID), clinical reports, and measurement annotations synchronized with your organization's designated CloudPACS server.
  • User Authentication Credentials:
    Username, professional email, and secure session tokens used to verify authorized access to your hospital or diagnostic center's tenant.
  • Network & System State (INTERNET, ACCESS_NETWORK_STATE):
    Technical connection status required to establish secure HTTPS/WSS communication with your PACS server and notify users of offline states.

3. How Information is Used

Collected information is used strictly to:

  • Provide authorized radiologists and clinicians with remote DICOM image viewing, manipulation (window/level, zoom, pan, measurements), and report review.
  • Convert dictation into text inside clinical report fields.
  • Enforce strict role-based access control (RBAC) and maintain regulatory audit trails of which medical practitioner accessed patient records.
  • Provide technical assistance, crash diagnostics, and platform maintenance.

4. AI Assistance (ProxoAI)

Where enabled by your healthcare provider, assistive decision-support tools (ProxoAI) analyze clinical findings within isolated, secure enterprise environments. Patient data is never used to train public foundational AI models. Final diagnosis, report verification, and clinical approval always remain with licensed medical professionals.

5. Data Security & Storage

Encryption & Protection: All data transmissions between the ProxoPACS mobile client and backend servers are secured using industry-standard TLS encryption. Medical records and DICOM binaries are encrypted at rest using AES-256 encryption.

6. Data Retention & Deletion Rights

We do not retain raw voice dictation recordings. Diagnostic images and patient records are retained according to the legal retention schedule designated by your healthcare provider.

Users may clear cached imaging files directly within the mobile application settings. To request the deletion of account credentials or clinical records, authorized users may contact their hospital system administrator or contact our privacy office at privacy@proxomind.com.

7. Children's Privacy

ProxoPACS is designed for use by authorized adult healthcare practitioners, hospital personnel, and radiologists. It is not marketed to or intended for direct use by children under the age of 18.

8. Changes to this Policy

We may periodically update this policy to reflect application enhancements or regulatory changes. Updated versions will be published at this URL with a revised effective date.
